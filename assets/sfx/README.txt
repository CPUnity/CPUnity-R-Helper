Sound effects of the Devil's Table (mp3, mono or stereo, any rate; short ones).

chips.mp3     chips handed to you: your stack put down when the first hand can be dealt, and every pot you win
shuffle.mp3   the deck being shuffled, as a hand begins
card1-4.mp3   a card put down on the felt (the burn cards, the flop, turn and river, and the hole cards);
              four takes of it, played in turn so no two in a row sound the same

To change a sound, replace its file and build. New effects are added in src/sfx.rs.
