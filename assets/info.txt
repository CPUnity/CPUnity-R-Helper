# The Continuation of Rebellion: CR-Helper

It is my fork of R-Helper by Fatalution: one small portable program that lets you run a Razer Blade without Razer Synapse getting in the way. No installer, nothing hidden. Every place it keeps data is listed in the **Files** tab, and you can open each one with a click.

## Where to start

- **Main:** pick a performance mode for when you are plugged in and one for battery, set the fan, and cap the battery charge.
- **Lighting:** keyboard and cooling pad effects. CR-Helper never touches your lights until you click something.
- **Sensors:** switch on **Read sensors** to see temperatures, power limits, clocks and load, with graphs and CSV logs you can look at afterwards in the **Log viewer**.
- **Files:** where everything lives, and where to tell CR-Helper to find the other programs it reads from. The PawnIO section has a **Get Modules Here** drop-down with the downloads.
- **Changelog:** what changed, version by version.

## Good to know

- For CPU temperature and power limits you need a source with a kernel driver: HWiNFO, PawnIO, LibreHardwareMonitor, or a ThrottleStop / XTU log. PawnIO needs administrator rights, and Options can start CR-Helper as administrator for you.
- Logging is always off when CR-Helper starts. It never records anything until you switch it on.
- Synapse Standby keeps your Synapse settings and can always be undone with **Resume**.
- If something misbehaves, open the diagnostic log from the **Files** tab and send it along.

# The Rebellion

Software that runs on your own machine should answer to you. Too many companies ship control software that reports back, runs when you never asked it to, and fights the settings you chose. Some people got tired of that and built the replacement themselves. Fatalution was one of them. R-Helper is a small program that does its job and nothing else: no account, no telemetry, nothing sent anywhere.

That is the rebellion, and it matters: ordinary people refusing to put up with software they detest, and building what they wanted in its place.

When Fatalution returned his Razer Blade, plenty of people figured R-Helper went with it. It didn't. I'm carrying it on, because a tool people built for themselves shouldn't die with one person's hardware. CR stands for the Continuation of Rebellion.

Besides, now I'm stuck facing Synapse problems and a failing vapor chamber like the rest of you. Whoop whoop!
